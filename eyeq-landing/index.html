Questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should a child's eyes be checked?

Adults are usually advised a yearly eye test; children with myopia a check every 6 months. For other children, the doctor or optometrist will suggest how often to return after the first visit. An eye test for school children can be booked at any time of the year. Searching for a children eye doctor near me? The form lets you choose "My child".

What does a comprehensive eye test involve?

A comprehensive eye examination Bangalore residents can book at Eye Q is carried out by qualified optometrists, with an eye doctor on site for a consultation. The steps depend on your age and the reason for your visit, and the team explains each one as it happens.

What does a myopia assessment involve?

A myopia assessment is a children's eye visit that looks at distance vision and how it changes between check-ups. The team talks with you and your child, and the doctor explains the options, including whether something such as orthokeratology Bangalore families read about suits your child, and how often to come back.

What does a diabetic eye screening involve?

It is a screening visit for adults with diabetes, carried out under the care of the eye doctor. The team explains each step at the visit and how often a repeat check is usually advised.

What should I bring to a contact lens fitting?

Bring your previous glasses or prescription, any contact lenses you currently use with their case, and earlier eye reports if you have them. Contact lenses are fitted on site by the team.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking a time is recommended so an optometrist and the eye doctor can be ready for you. You can book with the form on this page, by phone or on WhatsApp. For walk-in availability, please call the clinic.
