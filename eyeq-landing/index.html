Diabetic Eye Screening

What it is

Diabetic eye screening is a check of the retina, the light-sensitive layer at the back of the eye, for changes linked to diabetes.

Who it is for

Adults living with diabetes, and anyone whose doctor has advised a retina check. Your physician or eye doctor will suggest how often to come back.

What to expect at the visit

The team asks about your general health history, checks your vision, and the eye doctor examines the retina. The doctor explains each step before it begins and talks you through the findings. Please bring any recent reports from your physician.

Contact Lens Fitting, Computer Vision and Digital Eye Strain

What it is

A contact lens fitting matches a lens to your eyes and your routine. The optometrist measures your eyes, helps you choose a lens type, and shows you how to insert, remove and care for your lenses. The same visit covers computer vision and digital eye strain, which is about comfortable sight during long screen hours.

Who it is for

First-time wearers, existing wearers who want a refit, and people who feel tired eyes, headaches or blurred vision after long hours on a computer or phone.

What to expect at the visit

A talk about how and where you use your eyes, measurements by the optometrist, a trial of suitable lenses and clear instructions on handling and cleaning. For screen comfort, the optometrist can also suggest glasses and simple daily habits.
